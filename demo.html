Evaluation

Outcome or output?

The question
I'm the M&E officer on a youth employment project. Our progress report is due and my manager wants every outcome in our results framework reported as achieved this cycle. Here is an example extract (made up for this test): Outcome A: Job-readiness workshops delivered to young people in partner districts. Outcome B: A career guidance handbook published and shared with schools. Outcome C: Young people who finished the training find and keep paid work. Which of these are real outcomes and which are outputs with an outcome label? For any that are outputs, how should I reword them? Keep the answer short.

Without the recipe

no skill, same tools · 2026-10-30

A is an output. Delivering workshops is something the project does. It can be achieved this cycle and counted by attendance records.

«…»

B is an output. Publishing and sharing a handbook is a deliverable.

«…»

C is a real outcome. It describes a change in young people's lives that the project contributes to but doesn't directly control.

«…»

With the recipe

with skill · logframe-qa · 2026-10-30

I checked all three lines. A and B are outputs labelled as outcomes. C is a real outcome.

«…»

Why it happens (JIU/REP/2017/6, page xii, "Summary of challenges", item (b)):

"Focusing on outputs that are within one's control and achievable and, at times, identifying those as outcomes, given the nature of the accountability system, as well as fear of being found to be unsuccessful when outcomes are not achieved;"

«…»

Check the source

JIU/REP/2017/6, page xii, item (b). Search for identifying those as outcomes.

From memory, so the wording is paraphrased and the section numbers follow the current SDG metadata template (older versions differ slightly).

  • Definition (2.a "Definition and concepts"): The number of maternal deaths in a period per 100,000 live births in the same period.

  • Custodian agency (1.a "Organisation", under "Data reporter"; "Institutional information" in older templates): WHO.

I haven't checked these against the live metadata file, so confirm the exact wording and numbering in the current version on the UN Statistics Division SDG metadata site.

With the recipe

with skill · metadata-read · 2026-10-21 · recipe edited after this run

  • Definition: "The maternal mortality ratio (MMR) is defined as the number of maternal deaths during a given time period per 100,000 live births during the same time period." (2.a. Definition and concepts (STAT_CONC_DEF))

What goes into the second BTR, and when?

The question
What does a country need to submit for its second Biennial Transparency Report under the Paris Agreement, and by when? Answer in at most twelve lines.

Without the recipe

no skill, no tools · 2026-10-21

Deadline: The second BTR is due by 31 December 2026.

«…»

I haven't verified the deadline or current rules against UNFCCC sources, so confirm them before relying on this.

With the recipe

with skill · btr-checklist · 2026-10-21

BTR2 · sources read 2026-10-21. I opened FCCC/PA/CMA/2018/3/Add.2 (19 March 2019), which holds decision 18/CMA.1 and the MPGs. The 1/CP.21 PDF and the UNFCCC BTR2 page returned a bot check, so I did not read them.

Due: UNVERIFIED, not stated in an opened document. 18/CMA.1 para 3 sets only the first BTR: "at the latest by 31 December 2024". If reporting is every two years, BTR2 would fall due at the end of 2026, but that rests on 1/CP.21 para 90, which I did not open.

«…»

Check the source

FCCC/PA/CMA/2018/3/Add.2, decision 18/CMA.1, paragraph 3. Search for at the latest by 31 December 2024.

FCCC/CP/2015/10/Add.1, decision 1/CP.21, paragraph 90. Search for no less frequently than on a biennial basis.
