Claude for SDG is an independent community project by Harim Jung. It is not affiliated with Anthropic or the United Nations. Views are my own.

Every figure on this site is read from the UN SDG Global Database, the SDG Metadata Repository or the publisher's own report page and data file, and matched to a stored copy of the source before it is published.

Visits are counted with GoatCounter: no cookies, no personal profiles.
