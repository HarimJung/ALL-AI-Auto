1. Four steps, and the value is the third

Step Question Where What you may do with it
1 Frame What we agreed to measure SDG Indicators Home Name the code and title. No value
2 Define What the number counts Metadata Repository, one PDF per indicator Quote it with § and the edition date
3 Value The figure itself Global Database Cite it, with series code, year and Nature flag
4 Place What it means here UN Data Commons for the SDGs Read it. Never the cited figure

Why the order is not a habit

The UNSD Metadata Repository page states it directly: "Please note that no data are published in the Global SDG Indicators Database until the corresponding metadata is approved by the IAEG-SDGs." The same page says the timeline for review and approval can span up to 12 weeks or more. Definition comes before value because that is the order the value went through to reach the database.

The complete metadata set on that page is dated as of 1 October 2026. The file name is fixed: Metadata-<goal>-<target>-<indicator>.pdf, two digits each, zero padded. The Sources list below has three. Give Claude that URL. Do not ask it to find one.

The recorded run

Asked on 2026-10-21 at 02:46 UTC, with no tools: "What is the latest SDG indicator 4.7.1 value for Kenya?"

The answer opened with "I don't know a specific published 4.7.1 value for Kenya, and I won't make one up." It closed with "For the latest figure, check the UNESCO Institute for Statistics data browser or the UN SDG Global Database."

It did not invent a number. It still sent me to look for one figure. The 4.7.1 metadata (2026-03-27 edition) lists 8 series under that code in §0.d:

Survey component, SE_GCEDESD_ Greening score, SE_SGE_
NEP national education policies ENVSUST environment and sustainability
CUR curricula CLIMCH climate change
TED teacher education BIODIV biodiversity
SAS student assessment OVERALL all three areas

§4.c: "The component scores all lie between zero and one and are presented as a dashboard of four scores. They are not combined to create a single overall score for the indicator." §2.a: "It measures government intentions and not necessarily what is implemented in practice in schools and classrooms."

The 6.1.1 catch, in §4.c and §4.g

Metadata 6.1.1, 2024-09-27 edition. §4.c: "To calculate the proportion of the population using safely managed drinking water services, three ratios must be calculated". The country value is the minimum of the three. §4.g applies the same rule to regions: the value is calculated "by taking a minimum of the three elements, or of two elements if either accessibility or availability is missing." Read the value first and it looks like an average.

2. Four blocks, and what each one permits

Block What it holds Where Permission
1 Framework Codes, titles, targets SDG Indicators Home Name it. No value
2 Methodology What the number counts Metadata Repository Quote with § and date
3 Values The figure Global Database, SDG API Cite it
4 Context and owner Background, and who to ask Data Commons, Data Contacts Read it. Never cite it

Block 1. The global indicator framework was adopted by the General Assembly on 6 July 2017, in the resolution on the work of the Statistical Commission pertaining to the 2030 Agenda.

Block 3 has values you never see. 6.1.1 §4.f: regional and global estimates are calculated when non-imputed data cover at least 30 percent of the population using improved drinking water sources in the region. "Imputed country estimates are not published and only used for aggregation."

Block 4 has a person. UNSD Data Contacts lists a focal point for each indicator, nominated by the custodian, for questions on definitions, methods of computation and data. The page gives its next planned major update as December 2026.

The recorded run: placing a link

Asked on 2026-10-21 at 02:46 UTC, with no tools, using the prompt below and the UNICEF SDG data page. The answer in part:

"Block: 4 Context. I'm going from memory and haven't opened the page." "May I cite a number from it? Only alongside Block 3." "NOT OFFICIAL? No. UNICEF is a UN agency and a formal SDG custodian for those indicators, so this counts as an official source."

It said it had not opened the page. The block call matches how I use thematic portals. I compare against them and cite the Global Database.

3. The acronym map, three columns

Column 1 is the acronym as your team writes it. Column 2 is the official expansion, quoted. Column 3 is what it means for your team, and only you can fill it.

Acronym Official expansion What it means for us Source
PCCB Paris Committee on Capacity-building Climate side Decision 1/CP.21, paragraph 71
HLG-PCCB High-level Group for Partnership, Coordination and Capacity-Building for statistics for the 2030 Agenda Statistics side, created 6 March 2015 by the Statistical Commission UNSD HLG-PCCB page
ACE Action for Climate Empowerment Article 12 of the Paris Agreement Decision 17/CMA.1, paragraph 1
BTR biennial transparency report First one due by 31 December 2024 Decision 18/CMA.1, paragraph 3
MMR maternal mortality ratio Per 100 000 live births, SDG 3.1.1 Metadata 3.1.1 §4.b

One acronym, two decisions. The BTR deadline sits in 18/CMA.1, paragraph 3. The biennial part of the name comes from 1/CP.21, paragraph 90: Parties "shall submit the information" referred to in Article 13 "no less frequently than on a biennial basis", with discretion for least developed countries and small island developing States.

One pair, two denominators. 3.1.1 §4.b, 2025-11-04 edition: "The maternal mortality ratio (MMR) is defined as the number of maternal deaths during a given time period per 100 000 live births during the same time period." And: "By contrast, the maternal mortality rate (MMRate) is calculated as the number of maternal deaths divided by person-years lived by women of reproductive age."

4. Four climate branches, and Education first

For climate education officers. Two branches are two halves of the same report.

Branch What it settles Where it starts
1 Education What is measured, and the frame UNESCO ESD page, metadata 13.3.1
2 Process What is owed under the Paris Agreement Article 12, decision 17/CMA.1
3 Capacity Gaps and needs Paris Committee on Capacity-building, 1/CP.21 paragraph 71
4 Learning Courses that build the skills UN CC:Learn course catalogue

Education, the SDG half. UNESCO: "Education for sustainable development is an integral element of SDG target 4.7." The indicator is 13.3.1. Its metadata (Last updated 2026-03-27) opens with "Target 13.3: Improve education, awareness-raising and human and institutional capacity on climate change" and carries the same §2.a sentence as 4.7.1: it measures government intentions and not necessarily what is implemented in practice in schools and classrooms. "Regional aggregates are not calculated."

Process, the Paris half. Decision 17/CMA.1, adopted on 15 December 2018, recalls Article 12 of the Paris Agreement on climate change education, training, public awareness, public participation and public access to information. Paragraph 1: work under Article 12 "will also be referred to as Action for Climate Empowerment". Paragraph 3: the focal points nominated under Article 6 of the Convention also serve as the Action for Climate Empowerment focal points.

The seam is written down. The same decision acknowledges "the importance of linkages between activities taken to support Article 6 of the Convention, Article 12 of the Paris Agreement and the Sustainable Development Goals".

Why Education first. The 13.3.1 definition limits every results sentence that follows it. Read Process second so the Article 12 commitments are measured against what 13.3.1 can show.

Learning, with a date. The UN CC:Learn course catalogue showed 66 results in English when I checked on 2026-10-21. Counts move. Keep the date or drop the number.

The recorded run

Asked on 2026-10-21 at 02:47 UTC, no tools: "I am a climate change education officer in a UN country office. Which one official document should I read first, and why?" It answered the Paris Agreement, and: "Article 12 on climate change education, training, public awareness and access to information is the legal basis for your role. Read it alongside the UNFCCC's Action for Climate Empowerment (ACE) framework, which turns that article into practice." Correct for the Paris half. 13.3.1 was not in the answer.

This guide covers order and permission. It does not choose the right indicator for your report, and it does not replace the custodian focal point.
