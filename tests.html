W02Asked 2026-10-20 23:10 UTC

How does UNDP compute the Human Development Index from its three dimension indices, and what are the minimum and maximum goalposts for life expectancy? Answer in at most three sentences.

Claude, no skill, no tools

UNDP computes the HDI as the geometric mean of the three normalized dimension indices (health, education, and income): HDI = (I_health × I_education × I_income)^(1/3). Each dimension index is calculated as (actual value − minimum) / (maximum − minimum). For life expectancy at birth, the goalposts are a minimum of 20 years and a maximum of 85 years.

W01Asked 2026-10-20 21:24 UTC

How is the SDG 6.1.1 'safely managed drinking water services' figure built from its three service criteria? Answer in at most three sentences.

Claude, no skill, no tools

The JMP's "safely managed" drinking water figure is the share of the population using an improved source that meets all three criteria at once: it is located on premises, water is available when needed, and it is free from faecal (E. coli) and priority chemical contamination (arsenic and fluoride). In practice it's built as a "ladder" in which a household counts as safely managed only if it passes every criterion. The overall figure is therefore limited by the weakest criterion, and in data-sparse countries it is often estimated from whichever of the three have data, since water quality data are the scarcest.

Official source, metadata 6.1.1 §4.c
Safely managed drinking water services is taken as the minimum of these three indicators for any given year.
Matches metadata 6.1.1 §4.c: the lowest of the three
